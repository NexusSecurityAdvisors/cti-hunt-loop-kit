# Questions to Ask a Paid Intelligence or Tool Vendor

1. Can your intelligence be filtered by our PIRs, or only by keyword and actor?
2. Do your reports carry source grading and confidence language we can map to the Admiralty scale and ICD 203?
3. Can we export which of your products led to hunts or detections, so we can score you?
4. Can detections or queries you supply be exported in Sigma or another portable format?
5. What does your AI assistant do with our data and prompts, and how do you defend it against injected content?
6. What happens to our data, and our ability to export it, if we leave?
