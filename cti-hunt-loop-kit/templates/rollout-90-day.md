# 90-Day Rollout

No new headcount is needed to start. A realistic minimum is part of one CTI analyst's time and part of one hunter's time for the first quarter, plus a 60-minute monthly feedback review chaired by the CISO. Adjust to your environment.

| Phase | Days | Work | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 1 to 30 | Write 3 to 5 PIRs with decision owners; build the data inventory and collection plan; adopt the hypothesis card and preflight checklist; pick one pilot PIR | PIRs signed by their owners |
| Pilot | 31 to 60 | Run 3 or 4 hunts on the pilot PIR; record every outcome; raise collection requirements; ship the first hunt-derived detection | Every hunt has a recorded outcome |
| Report and extend | 61 to 90 | First source-scoring review; report the metrics scorecard; extend the loop to all PIRs; brief the board | Board brief delivered |
