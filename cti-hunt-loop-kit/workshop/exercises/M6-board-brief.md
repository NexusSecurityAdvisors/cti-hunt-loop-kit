# M6: Board Brief and Take-Home Plan (10 minutes)

**Goal:** a one-page brief for Halvard's board, and one PIR for your own organization.

## Tasks

1. (5 min) Fill in `templates/board-brief.md` for Halvard using your outcome log. Use the outcome names. Ask for one decision with an owner and a due date.
2. (5 min) Write one PIR for your own organization in a fresh copy of `templates/pir-register.csv`. Book 30 minutes next week to write its first hypothesis card.

## Take home

- The whole kit, under CC BY 4.0
- `templates/rollout-90-day.md` for the first quarter
- `templates/metrics-scorecard.csv` for reporting
