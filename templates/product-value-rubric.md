# Source Product-Value Rubric

Score every intelligence source (feed, vendor, report series, sharing community) once a month at the feedback review. Use the outcome log as evidence.

| Score | Meaning | Action for the CTI lead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Product led to no hunt | Ask whether it answers any PIR; consider dropping |
| 1 | Hunt run, no recorded outcome | Fix the process, not the product |
| 2 | Bounded non-observation, or inconclusive with a gap raised | Keep; it is shaping visibility |
| 3 | Supported finding or escalated incident | Keep and request more from that source |

**Source score** = mean score of all products from that source in the period. Report it with the number of products scored.

Two cycles of scores are usually enough to inform a renewal conversation. A low score is a prompt to check the PIRs as well as the source.
