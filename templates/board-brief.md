# One-Page Board Brief

Keep it to one page. Lead with the business question, not the activity.

| Section | Content |
| --- | --- |
| The question we answered | e.g. Are adversaries reaching our engineering data through cloud identity or OT remote access? |
| What we found | One line per hypothesis, using the outcome names: supported finding, bounded non-observation, inconclusive |
| What it means for the business | Exposure reduced, and blind spots that remain |
| Decision or support needed | Decision, owner, cost estimate, due date |
| Trend | PIRs answered this quarter vs. last; decisions closed vs. due; gaps closed vs. opened |

## Leave out

Indicator counts, feed volumes, alerts processed, and number of hunts run. None of them tell a director whether the company is harder to attack.

## Questions directors should ask

1. Which three business questions does our threat intelligence answer, and who owns each?
2. What did we learn this quarter that changed a decision or a control?
3. Where are we blind, and what would it cost to see?
4. Which intelligence sources do we pay for, and which ones led to action?
5. How would we know if an adversary were already inside our engineering or OT environment?
