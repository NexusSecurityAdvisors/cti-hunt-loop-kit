# M1: Writing PIRs a Hunter Can Use (40 minutes)

**Goal:** two priority intelligence requirements that a hunter could act on this week.

## Brief

Halvard's CIO asks: "Is anyone after our engineering data, and do we need to spend money on it this year?"

## Tasks

1. (10 min) Read `templates/pir-register.csv`. Note how each PIR names a **decision it informs** and a **decision owner**.
2. (15 min) Write two PIRs for Halvard: one about cloud identity, one about vendor access to the OT network. For each, fill in every column of the register.
3. (10 min) Swap with the next team. Score their PIRs with three questions:
   - Could a hunter tell what data to look at?
   - Is the decision specific enough that someone could say yes or no?
   - Is there a review date?
4. (5 min) Fix one thing the other team flagged.

## Common traps

- "Monitor for APTs" is a topic, not a requirement. Name the asset, the path, and the decision.
- A PIR without an owner is never reviewed.
